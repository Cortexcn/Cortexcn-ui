Security

Private by design, from the first upload

Privacy is the starting point, not a setting. Here is how your data is handled.

  • Encrypted storage

    Your files and campaigns are encrypted at rest and in transit between your browser and Lumio.

  • Private workspaces

    Each team works in its own workspace, and only invited people can see what is inside.

  • Regional hosting

    Choose to keep your data in the EU, UK, or US region that suits your team.

  • Your data stays yours

    Assets, prompts, and results are never used to train any model, ours or a provider's.

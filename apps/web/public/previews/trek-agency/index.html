About Cairn

We walk slowly, in small groups, with people who know the way home.

Cairn started in 2014 with one guide, one route, and six friends who wanted to see the Dolomites without a coach tour. We now run thirty-one routes on four continents, and the rules have not changed: no more than eight hikers per guide, local guides paid fairly, and days planned around the walk rather than the timetable.

What's included

We carry the logistics. You carry a day pack.

One price covers the walk and everything around it, from the airport pickup to the last dinner.

  • Expert local guides

    Every guide was born in the region they lead, holds a wilderness first aid certificate, and walks the route each season before you do.

  • Technical gear

    Poles, helmets, crampons, and sleeping bags, checked after every trip.

  • Door-to-trailhead transfers

    We meet you at the airport, drive you to the start, and take you back.

  • Mountain meals

    Breakfast and dinner at huts and inns, a packed lunch for the trail, and a stove for tea at the top.

  • Small groups

    Eight hikers per guide at most, so the pace fits the people.

  • Flexible dates

    Move your trek for free up to 60 days before you start.

How it works

From first click to first summit

Booking takes about ten minutes. After that, your guide and our planning team take care of the rest, and you get a clear plan for every day on the trail.

Plan your trek
  1. 01

    Choose a route

    Filter by terrain, length, and difficulty, or tell us what you have in mind and we will suggest two or three routes that fit.

  2. 02

    Reserve a place

    A 20% deposit holds your spot. The balance is due 60 days before you start, and you can move your dates for free until then.

  3. 03

    Get ready

    You get a packing list for your route, a training plan, and a video briefing with your guide two weeks before you fly.

  4. 04

    Walk with your guide

    We meet you at the airport. From there your guide handles the route, the weather calls, and the dinner tables.

  • Each trek has a difficulty rating. Easy and Moderate routes suit anyone who walks often and can manage five to six hours on foot with breaks. Challenging and Strenuous routes add longer climbs and some exposed ground, so we send a training plan when you book.

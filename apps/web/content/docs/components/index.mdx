Cortexcn components come in four families, and each family has its own section in the docs. Every one installs the same way: `npx shadcn@latest add @cortexcn/<name>`.

## Components

Everyday building blocks from shadcn/ui, from buttons and dialogs to menus, forms, and sidebars. Each page has the examples from the shadcn docs, ready to copy.

<ComponentsList category="Components" />

## Cortex

Cortexcn originals you won't find in shadcn/ui: motion, navigation, and theming. They have their own section, [Cortex](/docs/cortex).

<ComponentsList category="Cortex" />

## Backgrounds

Animated full-bleed backgrounds for heroes and sections, drawn with WebGL. They have their own section, [Backgrounds](/docs/backgrounds).

<ComponentsList category="Backgrounds" />

## Charts

Composable charts with animation, tooltips, and themes. Mix lines, areas, and time-aligned columns on one chart with the [**Composed Chart**](/docs/charts/composed-chart) (`SeriesBar` + `Line` / `Area`). They have their own section, [Charts](/docs/charts).

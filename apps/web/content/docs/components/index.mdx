Cortexcn components come in five families. Every one installs the same way: `npx shadcn@latest add @cortexcn/<name>`.

## Components

Everyday building blocks from shadcn/ui, from buttons and dialogs to menus, forms, and sidebars. Each page has the examples from the shadcn docs, ready to copy.

<ComponentsList category="Components" />

## Cortex

Cortexcn originals you won't find in shadcn/ui: motion, navigation, and theming.

<ComponentsList category="Cortex" />

## AI Assistant

Chat and agent interfaces for AI products: reasoning, chains of thought, and streaming states. They render the text and steps your model sends, from the AI SDK or any other source.

<ComponentsList category="AI Assistant" />

## Backgrounds

Animated full-bleed backgrounds for heroes and sections, drawn with WebGL.

<ComponentsList category="Backgrounds" />

## Charts

Composable charts with animation, tooltips, and themes. Mix lines, areas, and time-aligned columns on one chart with the [**Composed Chart**](/docs/components/composed-chart) (`SeriesBar` + `Line` / `Area`).

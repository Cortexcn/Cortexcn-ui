Cortexcn components come in three families. Every one installs the same way: `npx shadcn@latest add @cortexcn/<name>`.

## Neurons

Everyday building blocks, adapted from shadcn/ui and styled to match.

<ComponentsList category="Neurons" />

## Cortex

Cortexcn originals you won't find in shadcn/ui: motion, navigation, and theming.

<ComponentsList category="Cortex" />

## Charts

Composable charts with animation, tooltips, and themes. Mix lines, areas, and time-aligned columns on one chart with the [**Composed Chart**](/docs/components/composed-chart) (`SeriesBar` + `Line` / `Area`).
